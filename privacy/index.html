Privacy Policy

How Expressline LLC handles information when you use Axis.

Effective August 17, 2026

1. Who we are

Axis is provided by Expressline LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company. Privacy questions may be sent to privacy@tryaxis.so. General questions may be sent to contact@tryaxis.so.

2. Local-first product content

Axis runs on an Axis node you control. Your source files, project files, prompts, transcripts, commands, artifacts, credentials, and agent work are stored primarily on that node rather than in the Axis commercial control plane.

Content leaves your node when you direct Axis to send it to an AI provider, integration, paired device, or notification service, or when a feature clearly requires that transfer. Those third parties process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

3. Information Axis processes

  • Account and access information, such as email address, account identifiers, authentication state, plan, and billing state.
  • Installation and device information, such as pseudonymous device and installation identifiers, platform, architecture, app/runtime versions, entitlement state, and last-seen timestamps.
  • Security and reliability information, such as access IP history, coarse country, health heartbeats, update state, errors, and operational logs.
  • Aggregated usage and analytics, such as provider/model names, request and token counts, estimated provider cost, feature events, dates, and pseudonymous identifiers.
  • Billing evidence from payment providers, including customer, subscription, product, transaction, and webhook information.
  • Feedback and support messages you deliberately submit.
  • Legal acceptance evidence, including policy versions, acceptance time, account/authentication identifiers, and acceptance surface.
  • Paired-device and notification information, including device identity, token digests, notification preferences, push tokens, approval keys, titles, identifiers, approval text, and deep links needed to deliver enabled notifications.

4. Analytics and operational logs

Axis may use analytics to understand product usage, performance, reliability, and adoption. Axis does not use commercial analytics to intentionally collect source-code contents, prompt bodies, transcript bodies, project-file contents, secrets, or credentials.

Infrastructure logging may include request routes, pseudonymous runtime identifiers, timing and status information, error details, terminal command names, working-directory paths, and bounded SQL statement snippets. Do not place secrets in titles, paths, commands, or notification-visible text.

5. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, operate Axis, activate installations, provide access and updates, process billing, prevent fraud and abuse, diagnose failures, improve the product, provide support, enforce our Terms, and comply with law.

6. Providers and disclosures

We may use service providers for payments, email delivery, analytics, operational logging, hosting, push notifications, and other infrastructure. These may include Polar, Resend, PostHog, Apple Push Notification service, browser push providers, and infrastructure vendors. AI providers and integrations you configure receive the content you choose to send them.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, to investigate abuse, or in connection with a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for account security, service delivery, billing, support, fraud prevention, product improvement, and legal obligations. Retention varies by system and provider. Exact commercial access-IP observations expire after 30 days and are removed during security-history cleanup.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no system is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your computer, Axis node, provider credentials, API keys, and connected accounts.

8. Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion where applicable by emailing privacy@tryaxis.so. We may verify your identity and may retain information where required for security, billing, fraud prevention, backups, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. You may disable optional integrations or notifications through their available controls.

9. International use and children

Expressline LLC is based in the United States. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where providers operate. Axis is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as Axis changes. Material updates may require renewed acceptance. The version shown on this page identifies the policy currently in effect.
