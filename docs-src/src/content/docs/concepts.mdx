## 1. Context: start in the right operating environment

A **project** is the durable home for related work. It scopes sessions, files,
instructions, skills, work plans, services, Routines, Collections, and history.

**AGENTS.md** is the project's working agreement: standing rules every agent should
begin with automatically. **Memory** adds learned decisions and lessons relevant to the
request. Files and connected source systems remain authoritative for current state.

### Boundary card: rules, knowledge, and truth

| Surface | Job |
| --- | --- |
| AGENTS.md / project instructions | How work should be done here |
| Memory | What was learned and may matter again |
| Project files and connected systems | What is true now |
| Session history | What happened and what supports the result |

## 2. Continuity: keep work attached to its identity

A **session** is a continuing thread of work with one agent—not a disposable chat. It
holds the conversation, folded work, draft, status, and the surfaces opened for that
work.

A **workspace** is the session's coherent view: transcript plus tabs such as browser,
preview, Collection item, service shell, terminal, or agent graph. A pane stays attached
to the session that created it so evidence does not become an orphaned window.

A **workstream folder** groups related sessions and records purpose, decisions,
blockers, resources, and next action.

## 3. Control: make work and judgment visible

The **Working fold** presents one chronological rail for a turn. Actions disclose their
actual evidence when opened. The **steering queue** lets you redirect active work
without losing a message.

The **Inbox** collects questions, approvals, and other human actions that genuinely
block progress. An approval is a narrow authority boundary, not a generic "agent got
stuck" state.

### Boundary card: status has two layers

- Human-facing activity describes what you see now: working, waiting on you, or settled.
- Durable workflow status tracks lifecycle in records, such as backlog, todo,
  in-progress, review, done, or cancelled where that object supports those states.

Do not treat those two vocabularies as one universal enum.

## 4. Coordination: share intent and converge branches

A **work plan** is current shared intent that multiple sessions can continue. A
**task** is work waiting to begin or be resumed. A **sub-agent** is a focused branch of
a larger goal; the parent remains responsible for convergence and integration:

> **1 goal → N focused branches → 1 validated result**

A **Routine** is a reusable workflow. Scheduled, manual, or event-triggered runs should
create or resume visible sessions with history and the same approval boundaries as
interactive work.

## 5. Learning: keep what should outlive the turn

**Memory** preserves deliberate lessons, preferences, decisions, and references.
**Collections** is the project-scoped home for durable saved material; an artifact is a
saved item type, not a competing destination.

Evidence connects the entire model. A claim should lead to the exact changed state,
test, browser view, saved result, or recorded side effect that supports it.
